Videos

Everything caregivers have recorded. A video is private until the family's decision maker says otherwise, so "Ready to post" is the only pile you can use publicly.

Clients in the game

A client only appears for caregivers after you record their consent here. Each family gets one link plus two codes: the family code lets everyone watch and leave hearts, the decision maker code adds the yes-or-no on sharing.

Caregivers playing

Each caregiver gets one permanent private link. Text it to them once, they keep it on their phone. If someone leaves, switch them off and the link stops working.

The question bank

Whatever you star becomes the question of the week on every caregiver's screen. They can swap to any other one if it does not suit the person in front of them.

Scoreboard

1 point for recording a story, 5 every time a family member hearts it, 10 when a family says it may be shared. That way the win goes to whoever creates moments families love, not whoever films the most.
