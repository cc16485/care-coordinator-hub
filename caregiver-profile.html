Your caregiver profile

When we put you on a family's schedule, we introduce you first, with your photo and a little about you. Families tell us it is the difference between a stranger at the door and someone they were expecting. This takes about five minutes and you decide what goes in it.

Your name

Families see your first name only. Never your last name, address or phone number.

Your photo

A clear, friendly photo of your face. A phone selfie in good light is perfect.

A short video optional

Up to a minute, saying hello and why you do this work. Plenty of people would rather not be on camera, and that is genuinely fine, what you write below matters more.

Your experience

About you

Two or three sentences. What you are like to have in the house.
Stuck? People often say: how they are around the house (quiet, chatty, always cooking), what they are patient with, or what a good day looks like to them.
Most people have one story that started it. That is usually the answer.

Your permission

It is only ever sent to the family a caregiver is scheduled with, on a private link, never posted publicly and never given a search listing. You can change your mind at any time, just tell the office and it comes down.
