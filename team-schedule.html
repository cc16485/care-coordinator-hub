Each person's week at a glance

Krystal — Private Pay & LTC book

Mon–Fri 8am–2pm · field Tue & Thu 12–2
  • Her clients: every Private Pay, LTC-insurance, and PACE lead, client, and family. The hub auto-assigns them to her.
  • Owns the morning: overnight queue, lead callbacks, first-shift Care Match calls
  • Assessment scheduling before noon
  • Field route Tue/Thu: private-pay referral sources (discharge planners, home health, assisted living, physician offices), logged in Referrers same day

Angiel — Medicaid, VA & CDS book

Mon 10am–5pm · Tue–Fri 1pm–5pm · field Wed & Fri 2–4
  • Her clients: every Medicaid IHS and VA lead and client. The hub auto-assigns them to her.
  • CDS intakes are hers too: warm-transferred when she's in, saved to her book for callback when she's not. The intake itself runs on the CDS side and rides the CDS hub's tracker.
  • The monthly client check-ins are hers: the Client Check-ins board, Due This Week and Overdue at zero
  • The State Submissions board is hers: every DSDS referral chased weekly until authorized
  • Afternoon rhythm: follow-ups due, New Clients scheduling checklists, Needs Attention at zero by 5
  • Field route Wed/Fri: Medicaid & VA sources (DSDS case managers, veteran service officers, senior centers), logged same day

The one rule that makes this work: the board is the handoff. Nobody briefs anybody at shift change. Every record shows its stage, its notes, and its next follow-up date in the hub, so whoever is on works whatever is sitting in the queues. Ownership is a label, never a wall, and everyone sees everything. In practice: your book is yours to drive, but whoever is on shift answers for every client while they're on, a Private Pay family calling at 3pm gets helped by Angiel, then Krystal picks it up from the notes tomorrow.
Growth plan: when the Medicaid and VA books get big enough, they split, one coordinator for each. The payer-based structure is built for that day: the map just gains a name.

The field expectation, in writing

We get busy and the marketing is the first thing that gets skipped. So the field blocks are not a goal, they are part of the job, the same as answering the phone:
  • Two field blocks each, every week, at the set times. They are appointments with the business. Treat them like client visits.
  • Only Samantha can cancel a block, and a canceled block gets rescheduled inside the same week, never skipped. "It got busy" moves a block, it does not delete it.
  • Every visit is logged in the hub's Referrers tab before the shift ends. A visit that is not logged did not happen.
  • The scoreboard keeps the score, not memory. The Referrers page shows each person's count against 2, all week. From Wednesday on, anyone behind pace shows up red on Samantha's screen automatically.
  • Monday's huddle opens with last week's numbers, out loud. One short week is life. A pattern is a conversation.
