Client folders: what goes in, how to put them together, and how to order more

Every prospective private-pay client gets a folder at the in-home assessment. Pick the folder for the level of care you recommend. The printed pieces are ordered in bulk from Vistaprint, and you put each folder together from the boxes in the office.

1. Pick the folder for their level

Wellness Care

Level 1: company, meals, errands, light housekeeping

Open the Wellness folder

Personal Care

Level 2: hands-on help with bathing, dressing, moving safely

Open the Personal folder

Advanced Care

Level 3: higher needs, more complex care

Open the Advanced folder

Each one is the whole folder in pocket order (16 pages, 8 double-sided sheets). Use it to see what goes in, or to print one in a pinch if a box runs out.

2. Put the folder together

Left pocket · helping the family decide
  1. Our Levels of Care
  2. The sheet for their level: Wellness, Personal or Advanced Care
  3. Rates & Ways to Pay
  4. Meet Your Caregiver
Right pocket · getting started
  1. Client Handbook (the stapled booklet),
  2. Personalized Care Proposal, filled in after the assessment
  3. Next Steps
  4. Seeing Care Notes & Invoices in AxisCare
  5. The Caring Companions App
  1. Before the visit: put the left pocket together for the level you expect, and the right pocket (all five pieces). Every piece is one double-sided sheet except the handbook booklet, so nothing needs stapling.
  2. Add one extra flyer when it fits the family (see the list below), in the left pocket behind their level sheet.
  3. At the assessment, fill in the Care Proposal (level, days, hours, estimated weekly cost, start date) and put it in the right pocket.
  4. If they say yes, sign the Client Care Agreement on the tablet (the client's card in the Hub, Start of Care), or send it to sign from home. Use the paper agreement only as a backup.
Add when it fits the family: Coming Home from the Hospital (just home from a hospital or rehab) · Overnight, 24-Hour & Respite Care (nights, around the clock, or a worn-out family caregiver) · Long-Term Care Insurance & VA Benefits (a policy or a veteran) · Medicare GUIDE (dementia) · HomeTogether TV half-sheet (20+ hours a week) · Good Days Out (outings) · Caring CDS half-sheet (Missouri Medicaid, hire their own caregiver).

4. How to order more from Vistaprint

Paying: use the office card saved in the Caring Companions Vistaprint account. Never write a card number in the Hub, an email or a text. Ask Samantha if you don't have the Vistaprint sign-in.

Reorder something you've ordered before (easiest)

  1. Go to vistaprint.com and sign in to the office account.
  2. Open your account's past orders and find the piece.
  3. Choose Reorder, check the quantity, and check out with the saved card.

Only reorder a piece if this page shows the same Rev. date as the last order. If a piece changed, order it new with the PDF from this page.

Order a flyer new

  1. On vistaprint.com, open Postcards & Print Advertising, then under Marketing Materials choose Flyers. Pick 8.5" x 11".
  2. Pick 2-sided or 1-sided (the table above says which), and a matte paper, thicker if you can.
  3. Choose to upload your design and upload the PDF from this page. A 2-page PDF is the front and back.
  4. In the preview, check the front and back look right and nothing is cut off. The white edge around the page is meant to be there.
  5. Pick the quantity, then check out with the saved card. Ship to the office.

Order Client Handbooks

  1. On vistaprint.com, open Postcards & Print Advertising, then under Booklets, Catalogs & Magazines choose Saddle-Stitch Booklets (the stapled kind). Pick letter size (8.5" x 11") if offered.
  2. Pick 24 pages and upload 16-client-handbook-booklet.pdf from this page.
  3. Check the preview page by page, then check out with the saved card.

Order pocket folders

  1. On vistaprint.com, open Postcards & Print Advertising, then under Marketing Materials choose Folders. Pick 9" x 12" (holds letter-size sheets).
  2. Add the Caring Companions logo, or ask Samantha for the folder design.
  3. The smallest order is 100. Check out with the saved card.
When to reorder: when a box gets down to about 10, order the amount in the "Keep on hand" column. Orders take a few days to arrive, so don't wait until the last one.
When something changes (rates, the app, a policy), Samantha will update the PDFs here. Recycle the old ones and order the changed pieces only. The folder checklist says which pieces carry prices.

Prices change, so check the cart total before you pay. These PDFs are built from the Caring Companions marketing source (cc-folder-inserts) and are the only versions to order from.
