Thanksgiving 2026 thank-you

A thank-you from Samantha. Active families get a personal email, which Samantha sends. Past families get a mailed card that she signs. Never a deceased client's family, and no one marked do-not-contact. Both point to the star page first, so only 4 or 5 stars get offered Google.

Email to active families

Sent by Samantha from her own email, one family at a time. Suggested send day: Monday, November 23.

Subject: Thank you, from all of us at Caring Companions Hi {first name}, As Thanksgiving gets close, I wanted to take a minute to say thank you. Letting someone into your home to care for a person you love takes a lot of trust, and our whole team is grateful you chose us. If there is anything we could be doing better, or a caregiver you would like us to thank, just reply to this email or call me at (417) 234-8494. I read every message myself. And if you have one minute, I would love to know how we are doing: mo-care.com/feedback Wishing you and your family a warm and peaceful Thanksgiving. Samantha Troutman Co-Founder, Caring Companions (417) 234-8494 · mo-care.com
